# 🚀 Launching FLARE: Decentralized Disaster Response & Geospatial Geofencing

During critical crises and natural disasters, centralized communication networks and dispatch pipelines are often the first to fail. That is why I engineered **FLARE** — an offline-first, decentralized disaster response and resource coordination platform designed to function under extreme operational constraints.

### 🛠️ What I Built & Deployed:
• **Multi-Tier Crisis Command Decks**: 
  - **HQ Command Deck**: Real-time incident telemetry, macro asset metrics, and live broadcast dispatch.
  - **Logistics Deck**: High-velocity supply chain balancing, medical kit tracking, and automated inventory redistribution.
  - **Field Responder Deck**: Lightweight, battery-optimized mobile interface for on-the-ground volunteer check-ins and emergency SOS triggers.
• **Role Gateway Architecture**: Seamless role-switching between Incident Commander, Logistics Coordinator, and First Responder with zero session fragmentation.
• **Geospatial Hazard Intelligence**: Dynamic danger-zone geofencing, cluster heatmaps, and safe route corridor calculations.
• **Failsafe Broadcast & SOS Protocol**: High-priority alert broadcasting with an instant 5-second undo grace period to eliminate false alarms in high-stress scenarios.
• **Offline Resilience Engine**: CRDT-inspired state synchronization and local in-memory fallback allowing uninterrupted operations even during full network blackouts.
